Cattle Feed Optimizer is an AI-powered solution designed to assist farmers in providing scientifically tailored daily feed recommendations for their livestock based on breed, weight, age, purpose, and weather conditions. By integrating local market prices, it also estimates the daily feed cost, helping farmers make informed, budget-conscious decisions. This project addresses a critical gap in rural farming where feed planning is often based on guesswork, leading to poor productivity and high costs. With a user-friendly interface and data-driven insights, it empowers farmers to optimize livestock health and yield, ultimately improving livelihoods. More than just a tool, this project holds emotional value as it stands in solidarity with the farming community—offering them modern solutions rooted in empathy, efficiency, and empowerment.
